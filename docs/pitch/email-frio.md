**Subject:** MUSA — complete digital presence for museums, live and measured

Hi [name],

invisible: agencies sell them websites that die, and generic platforms don't
understand collections. MUSA fixes this as a service — a website with a
searchable catalog, interactive 3D, and an AI assistant that answers
visitors citing each piece's official record. The collection remains
legally the museum's, in an open format.

This isn't slideware: the product is built and proven end-to-end on a
public demo museum. Publishing a new piece takes 36 seconds (measured
median); the demo holds 455 records with public license and source, WCAG AA
accessibility, and LGPD compliance from day one. In Brazil alone the
addressable market is 3,902 mapped museums (Museus BR/Ibram, 2024), most
with no technical staff.
