# MUSA in plain language — for partners, investors and the curious

> Overview document (2026-10). No technical jargon; where a technical term is
> unavoidable, it comes explained. Every number quoted here was measured on
> the real product, not projected. Portuguese version: `visao-geral.md`.

## What MUSA is

MUSA is a **service that gives any museum a complete digital presence** — at
a level currently reserved for the world's largest institutions. The museum
hands us its collection (photos, record cards, 3D models); we deliver and
operate:

- an **elegant website** with a searchable catalogue;
- **interactive 3D models** of the pieces, spinnable on a visitor's phone;
- an **AI assistant** that knows every piece and answers visitor questions,
  citing the official record card as its source;
- depending on the plan: **online store, art-cinema streaming, a digital
  twin of the venue's rooms**, and integration with the museum's physical
  kiosks.

The museum never needs to hire a developer: publishing a new piece is a
two-minute gesture in a simple panel, and it is live immediately.

## The problem

Brazil and the world have thousands of small and mid-size museums with
valuable collections and a nonexistent or precarious digital presence. The
current options are bad: agencies charge heavily for a site that ages and
dies; generic platforms don't understand collections (record cards, image
licensing, item tiers); and bespoke projects don't scale.

## How it works (no tech)

Think of MUSA as a **digital franchise**: the experience is centralized and
kept up to date by us; the content belongs entirely to the museum.

1. **The collection stays in the museum's home.** Each client gets its own
   repository with its cards and images, in an open, documented format. If
   they ever want to leave, they take everything — no lock-in.
2. **The engine is ours and singular.** Every museum runs the same
   application, updated centrally. A new museum goes live in days, not months.
3. **Plans unlock capabilities.** Bronze is the complete digital museum;
   Silver adds interactive 3D and AI; Gold mirrors the entire venue online.
   Switching plans is instant — technically it is a renewed digital
   signature on one file, and the new capabilities simply start existing.
4. **AI works on the intake side too.** Photographing a paper record card is
   enough for MUSA to read it (OCR), structure the fields and prepare the
   digital card for human review — the historical bottleneck of
   digitization.

## Differentiators (selling points)

- **Publishing measured in seconds**: from the curator to the public, a new
  piece is live in under a minute (measured median: 36 s).
- **Near-zero marginal cost per museum**: the architecture (static sites +
  low-cost cloud infrastructure) was designed so that serving the 50th
  museum costs almost the same as serving the 2nd — that is what sustains
  the plan margins.
- **Commercial trust through architecture**: the collection is legally and
  technically the museum's (open format, full export at any time) — the
  opposite of the data captivity the sector fears.
- **Auditable quality**: every product version passes an automated gate that
  includes a "misuse battery" — we simulate a client doing everything wrong
  to prove nothing is lost.
- **Compliance from day one**: LGPD (the museum is the data controller, we
  are the processor), WCAG AA accessibility, and per-item credits and
  licenses displayed publicly on each record card.

## Service format

Monthly subscription per museum, in three plans (Bronze R$ 597, Silver
R$ 1,197, Gold R$ 3,197) plus add-on modules (AI assistant, store,
streaming, extended digital twin, data API, multilingual layer). The museum
contracts its own cloud infrastructure (cents at the scale of a typical
collection) — or, in the future, may opt for everything bundled into the
subscription.

3D scanning of pieces is the only on-site service: done by certified
partners, feeding the Silver/Gold plans.

## Where we are

The product is built and proven end to end with a public demo museum:

5-minute demonstration narrative for anyone who wants to watch it all work
live. Next step: the first client museum in production.
